METHODOLOGY AND DATA

How the recommendations work

Your address is matched to available regional boundaries. Documented local native-status records are used where supported; otherwise results use Ontario status and are labelled accordingly. Plants with missing information for a selected garden condition are excluded. Some growing conditions still require verification. A match is a preliminary gardening suggestion, not a professional planting prescription.

Sun, soil, moisture and irrigation are information that you provide. The map does not automatically infer them from your address. Different habitats can exist within a single neighbourhood.

Plant attributes are simplified from the sources linked on each card, including Watersheds Canada, Ontario Native Plants and Toronto municipal planting resources. Confirm species-level local origin, appropriate planting conditions, mature size, and nursery provenance before planting.

Address text is sent to OpenStreetMap’s public Nominatim geocoder when you press “Find garden”; application logs omit address text, and results are held in a bounded in-memory cache for up to 15 minutes. Hosting providers must configure their own logs accordingly. The built-in Oakville demo does not send a geocoding request. Nominatim usage policy.
